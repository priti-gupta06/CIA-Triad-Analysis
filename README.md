CIA Triad in Cybersecurity

The CIA Triad is a basic cybersecurity model used to protect information and systems.
It consists of three principles:

- Confidentiality – Protects information from unauthorized access.
- Integrity – Ensures information remains accurate and unchanged.
- Availability – Ensures information and services are available when needed.

Real-World Examples

- 🏦 Banking: Protects customer and transaction data.
- 🏥 Healthcare: Protects patient records.
- 🛒 E-Commerce: Protects customer and order information.
- 🎓 Education: Protects student records and marks.
- ☁️ Cloud Computing: Protects stored data and services.

Importance

The CIA Triad helps to:
- Protect sensitive data
- Prevent unauthorized changes
- Keep services available
- Reduce cybersecurity risks

Conclusion

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability are the three important principles of cybersecurity. 
Together, they help keep information secure, accurate, and accessible.
